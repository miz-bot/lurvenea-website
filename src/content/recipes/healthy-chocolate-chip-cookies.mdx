ingredients:
  - "1 1/2 cups white whole wheat flour"
  - "1/2 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/2 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened"
  - "1/3 cup coconut sugar"
  - "1/4 cup maple syrup"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "3/4 cup dark chocolate chips"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper."
  - "Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together in a medium bowl."
  - "In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, coconut sugar, and maple syrup until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes."
  - "Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until combined."
  - "Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix on low speed just until no flour streaks remain."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips by hand."
  - "Scoop 1.5-tablespoon portions onto the prepared sheets, spacing them 2 inches apart."
  - "Bake for 9–11 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers still look slightly underdone."
  - "Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack."

I started making these after realizing our usual chocolate chip cookie recipe was basically candy with a bit of flour holding it together. The goal here wasn't a "diet cookie" — it was a cookie that still tastes like a treat, just built on slightly better ingredients: whole wheat flour instead of all-purpose, maple syrup standing in for some of the sugar, and a shorter bake time that keeps every batch soft in the middle.

The dough comes together in one bowl with no mixer required, and there's no chilling step, so you can go from craving to warm cookie in about 25 minutes.
