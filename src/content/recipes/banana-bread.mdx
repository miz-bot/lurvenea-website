ingredients:
  - "3 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)"
  - "1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted"
  - "1/2 cup brown sugar"
  - "2 large eggs, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/2 tsp cinnamon"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C) and line a 9x5 loaf pan with parchment."
  - "In a bowl, whisk the mashed bananas with the melted butter and brown sugar until combined."
  - "Whisk in the eggs and vanilla until smooth."
  - "Add the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt, and fold with a spatula just until no dry flour remains."
  - "Pour into the loaf pan and smooth the top."
  - "Bake 50-60 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean."
  - "Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then lift out and cool before slicing."
whyItWorks:
  - "Very ripe, spotty bananas bring natural sweetness and moisture, so you need less sugar."
  - "Melted butter and a whisk mean no mixer — the batter comes together in one bowl."
  - "Folding the flour in just until combined keeps the crumb tender instead of dense."
  - "A little cinnamon deepens the flavor without overpowering the banana."
substitutions:
  - "Add-ins: fold in 1/2 cup chocolate chips or chopped walnuts."
  - "Healthier: swap half the flour for whole wheat flour."
  - "Dairy-free: use melted coconut oil instead of butter."
  - "Extra moist: add 1/4 cup sour cream or yogurt with the wet ingredients."
tips:
  - "The riper and spottier the bananas, the sweeter and moister the loaf."
  - "Don't overmix once the flour goes in, or the bread turns dense."
  - "Tent with foil if the top browns too fast before the center is done."
  - "Store wrapped at room temperature up to 4 days, or freeze slices up to 3 months."

This easy banana bread is moist, tender, and made in one bowl with just a whisk — no mixer needed. Really ripe, spotty bananas are the secret to natural sweetness and a soft crumb, and whisking the wet ingredients smooth before folding in the flour keeps it light. It comes together in about ten minutes of prep.
