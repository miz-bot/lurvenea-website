ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted"
  - "1 cup granulated sugar"
  - "2 large eggs, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1/2 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional)"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C) and line an 8x8 pan with parchment, leaving an overhang."
  - "Whisk the melted butter and sugar together until smooth."
  - "Whisk in the eggs and vanilla vigorously for about a minute, until glossy and slightly thickened — this makes the crackly top."
  - "Add the flour, cocoa, and salt, and fold just until no dry streaks remain."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips if using."
  - "Spread in the pan and bake 20-25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs."
  - "Cool before lifting out and cutting into squares."
whyItWorks:
  - "Melted butter and cocoa powder mean no mixer and no melting chocolate — the batter comes together in one bowl with a whisk."

  - "Pulling them at moist crumbs, not a clean toothpick, keeps the centers fudgy."
  - "Simple pantry ingredients make these an easy from-scratch swap for a box mix."
substitutions:

  - "Chewier: swap 1/4 cup of the granulated sugar for brown sugar."
  - "Dairy-free: use vegan butter."
tips:
  - "Don't overbake — moist crumbs on the toothpick means fudgy centers."
  - "The parchment overhang lets you lift the whole slab out for clean squares."
  - "Room-temperature eggs whisk in smoother for a glossier top."
  - "Store airtight up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months."

These easy brownies from scratch prove you don't need a box mix or a mixer — just one bowl, a whisk, and simple pantry ingredients. They come out rich, fudgy, and shiny on top every time. A mix of melted butter and cocoa keeps it quick, and whisking the eggs and sugar until glossy is the trick behind that crackly top. If you've been buying boxed, these homemade brownies from scratch are just as easy and far better.
