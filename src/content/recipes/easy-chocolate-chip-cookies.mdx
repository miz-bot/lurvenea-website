ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled"
  - "1/2 cup brown sugar"
  - "1/4 cup granulated sugar"
  - "1 large egg plus 1 yolk, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1/2 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "3/4 cup chocolate chips"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C) and line a baking sheet with parchment."
  - "Whisk the melted butter with both sugars until smooth and glossy."
  - "Whisk in the egg, yolk, and vanilla until the mixture looks silky, about 30 seconds."
  - "Add the flour, baking soda, and salt, and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips."
  - "Scoop into 18 balls and space them on the sheet."
  - "Bake 9-11 minutes, until edges are set but centers still look soft."
  - "Cool on the sheet 5 minutes before moving."
whyItWorks:
  - "Melting the butter means no mixer and no waiting for it to soften, so the dough comes together in one bowl with a whisk."
  - "Whisking the butter, sugar, and egg until glossy gives an even, tender crumb."
  - "The extra yolk adds chew and richness without making them cakey."
  - "Pulling them while the centers look underdone keeps them soft as they set on the pan."
substitutions:
  - "Brown butter: brown the butter first for a deeper, nutty flavor."
  - "Dark chocolate: swap chips for chopped 70% dark chocolate."
  - "Gluten-free: use a 1:1 gluten-free baking flour."
  - "Add nuts: fold in 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans."
tips:
  - "Room-temperature egg blends in smoother and keeps the melted butter from clumping."
  - "Slightly underbake for soft centers; they firm up as they cool."
  - "Press a few extra chips on top of each ball before baking for the bakery look."
  - "Store airtight up to 5 days, or freeze the dough balls and bake from frozen (add 1-2 minutes)."

These easy chocolate chip cookies come together in one bowl with just a whisk — no mixer, no chilling, no fuss. Soft and chewy in the middle with lightly crisp edges, they use simple pantry ingredients and are ready in under 25 minutes. Melting the butter and whisking it smooth with the sugars is the shortcut that makes them so quick.
