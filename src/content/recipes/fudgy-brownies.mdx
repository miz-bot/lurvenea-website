ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter"
  - "6 oz dark chocolate (70%), chopped"
  - "3/4 cup granulated sugar"
  - "1/4 cup light brown sugar"
  - "2 large eggs plus 1 egg yolk, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1/2 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "1/2 cup dark chocolate chunks (optional)"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8 pan with parchment, leaving an overhang."
  - "Melt the butter and chopped chocolate together until smooth, then let cool slightly."
  - "Whisk in both sugars until fully combined."
  - "Whisk in the eggs, yolk, and vanilla vigorously for about a minute, until the batter looks glossy and slightly thickened — this is what creates the shiny, crackly top."
  - "Add the flour, cocoa, and salt, and fold with a spatula just until no streaks remain. Do not overmix."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chunks, then spread the batter evenly in the pan."
  - "Bake 22–28 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs (not wet batter, not clean)."
  - "Cool completely in the pan before lifting out and cutting into squares."
whyItWorks:
  - "Melting whole dark chocolate into the butter — instead of relying on cocoa alone — is what makes these dense and fudgy rather than cakey."
  - "Whisking the eggs and sugar vigorously until glossy dissolves the sugar and whips in air that sets into the signature shiny, crackly top. No mixer needed — just a whisk and a minute of effort."
  - "Pulling them out when the toothpick shows moist crumbs keeps the centre fudgy; a clean toothpick means they're overbaked."
  - "A little brown sugar adds chew and a deeper caramel note."
substitutions:
  - "Gluten-free: swap the flour 1:1 for a gluten-free baking blend."
  - "Extra fudgy: use 3 egg yolks and just 1 whole egg."
  - "Dairy-free: use vegan butter and dairy-free dark chocolate."
  - "Add-ins: fold in 1/2 cup chopped walnuts with the chocolate chunks."
tips:
  - "Room-temperature eggs whisk in smoother and trap more air for a better top."
  - "The parchment overhang lets you lift the whole slab out for clean squares."
  - "For bakery-clean cuts, chill 20 minutes and wipe the knife between cuts."
  - "Store airtight up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months."

These fudgy brownies have a shiny, crackly top and a dense, gooey chocolate centre — and they come together in one bowl with just a whisk, no electric mixer. Melting real dark chocolate into the butter is the secret to that deep, fudgy texture, and a good vigorous whisk is all it takes to get the glossy, crackly top bakeries are known for.
