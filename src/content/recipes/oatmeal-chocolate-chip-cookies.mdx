ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled"
  - "1/2 cup brown sugar"
  - "1/4 cup granulated sugar"
  - "1 large egg plus 1 yolk, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1 1/2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats"
  - "1/2 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/2 tsp cinnamon"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "3/4 cup chocolate chips or chopped dark chocolate"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C) and line a baking sheet with parchment."
  - "Whisk the melted butter with both sugars until smooth and glossy."
  - "Whisk in the egg, yolk, and vanilla until silky."
  - "Add the flour, oats, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt, and stir with a spatula just until combined."
  - "Fold in the chocolate."
  - "Scoop into 20 balls, space on the sheet, and flatten slightly."
  - "Bake 10-13 minutes, until edges are set but centers still look soft."
  - "Cool on the sheet 5 minutes before moving."
whyItWorks:
  - "Melted butter and a whisk mean no mixer, so the dough comes together in one bowl."
  - "Old-fashioned rolled oats give that hearty, chewy oatmeal-cookie texture; quick oats would make them softer and less defined."
  - "Brown sugar adds moisture and a caramel note that keeps them soft for days."
  - "Pulling them while the centers look underdone locks in a chewy middle."
substitutions:
  - "Oatmeal raisin style: swap half the chocolate for raisins."
  - "Gluten-free: use certified GF oats and a 1:1 gluten-free flour."
  - "Nutty: fold in 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans."
  - "Dairy-free: use coconut oil and dairy-free chocolate."
tips:
  - "Old-fashioned oats, not instant, give the best chew."
  - "Slightly underbake for soft centers; they firm up as they cool."
  - "Press a few extra chocolate pieces on top of each ball before baking."
  - "Store airtight up to 5 days, or freeze the dough balls and bake from frozen (add 1-2 minutes)."

These oatmeal chocolate chip cookies are soft, chewy, and made in one bowl with just a whisk — no mixer needed. If you love a classic oatmeal cookie but want melty chocolate instead of raisins, these chocolate chip oatmeal cookies are it: hearty rolled oats, warm brown sugar, and plenty of dark chocolate. Whisking the melted butter and sugars smooth is the shortcut that keeps them quick.
