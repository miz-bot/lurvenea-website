ingredients:
  - "1 cup creamy peanut butter"
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened"
  - "1/2 cup brown sugar"
  - "1/2 cup granulated sugar, plus extra for rolling"
  - "1 large egg, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1/2 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F (175C) and line a baking sheet with parchment."
  - "Whisk the peanut butter, softened butter, and both sugars until smooth and creamy."
  - "Whisk in the egg and vanilla until combined."
  - "Add the flour, baking soda, and salt, and stir with a spatula just until a soft dough forms."
  - "Roll into 20 balls, roll each in a little granulated sugar, and space on the sheet."
  - "Press a criss-cross pattern into each with a fork."
  - "Bake 9-11 minutes, until the edges are just set; the centers stay soft."
  - "Cool on the sheet 5 minutes before moving."
whyItWorks:
  - "Whisking the peanut butter with the butter and sugars first means no mixer and a smooth, even dough."
  - "A mix of brown and white sugar keeps them soft and chewy with a light crisp edge."
  - "The fork press isn't just classic looks; it flattens the dense dough so they bake evenly."
  - "Pulling them when the edges just set keeps the centers soft."

These peanut butter cookies are soft, chewy, and packed with rich peanut butter flavor — with that classic criss-cross fork pattern on top. They come together in one bowl with just a whisk, no mixer, and a short ingredient list. Whisking the peanut butter, butter, and sugars smooth is all it takes before the flour goes in.
