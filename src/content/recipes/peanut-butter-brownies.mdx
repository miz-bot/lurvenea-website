ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup unsalted butter"
  - "6 oz dark chocolate (70%), chopped"
  - "3/4 cup granulated sugar"
  - "1/4 cup light brown sugar"
  - "2 large eggs plus 1 egg yolk, room temperature"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1/2 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder"
  - "1/4 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "1/3 cup creamy peanut butter, warmed until pourable"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8 pan with parchment, leaving an overhang."
  - "Melt the butter and chopped chocolate together until smooth, then let cool slightly."
  - "Whisk in both sugars until combined."
  - "Whisk in the eggs, yolk, and vanilla vigorously for about a minute, until glossy and slightly thickened — this creates the crackly top."
  - "Add the flour, cocoa, and salt, and fold with a spatula just until no streaks remain."
  - "Spread the batter in the pan. Drop spoonfuls of the warmed peanut butter over the top and swirl with a knife for a marbled effect."
  - "Bake 22–28 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs."
  - "Cool completely in the pan before lifting out and cutting into squares."
whyItWorks:
  - "Melting whole dark chocolate into the butter makes these dense and fudgy rather than cakey."
  - "Whisking the eggs and sugar until glossy sets up the shiny, crackly top — no mixer required, just a whisk."
  - "Warming the peanut butter first makes it pourable so it swirls cleanly instead of clumping."
  - "Pulling them when the toothpick shows moist crumbs keeps the centre fudgy."
substitutions:
  - "Crunchy PB: use crunchy peanut butter for texture."
  - "Gluten-free: swap the flour 1:1 for a gluten-free baking blend."
  - "Nut-free: swap peanut butter for sunflower seed butter (same technique)."
  - "Salted top: sprinkle a little flaky sea salt over the swirl before baking."
tips:
  - "Natural peanut butter can be oily; a no-stir creamy PB swirls most reliably."
  - "Warm the peanut butter 15–20 seconds so it ribbons off the spoon."
  - "The parchment overhang lets you lift the whole slab out for clean squares."
  - "Store airtight up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months."

These peanut butter brownies are everything a fudgy brownie should be — dense, gooey, deeply chocolatey — with a glossy swirl of peanut butter marbled across the crackly top. They come together in one bowl with just a whisk, no mixer. Melting real dark chocolate into the butter gives them that fudgy base, and a quick swirl of warm peanut butter on top bakes into a beautiful marbled finish.
